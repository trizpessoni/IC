# Between Itamaraty and the Digital Public Square

Research project on Brazilian foreign policy, social media, and public opinion during the 2022 presidential election.

The corpus consists of posts from the official X accounts of Lula (@LulaOficial) and Jair Bolsonaro (@jairbolsonaro) published between October 3 and 29, 2022.

The original X/Twitter archive was processed with ContextBolt, resulting in a total of 987 posts gathered with X's bookmark feature. Unfortunally, the bookmarks are not available in the zip. Nontheless, after a qualitative screening, 130 posts were selected for in-depth analysis. Those 130 posts are in the archive, following: data -> like.js
